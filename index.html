About

From the Shop Floor to the Dashboard

Stay Curious is more than a motto — it is the belief that data from the past can help us make wiser decisions and shape a stronger future, and it is the thread running through everything I do.

I began my career on the production floor, commissioning equipment, troubleshooting controls, and analyzing plant performance as a Data Analyst and Electrical Engineer at Reliance Industries Limited. That work included data-driven improvements that increased production capacity by 15 percent, and it created a lasting curiosity about solving problems through data rather than intuition alone.

I bring both disciplines to every engagement: a hands-on engineering foundation in PLC troubleshooting, root cause analysis, IATF 16949 quality systems, and plant debottlenecking, alongside a data practice built in SQL, Power BI, Tableau, and Python. NPower's Junior Data Analyst Program, Microsoft AI-900 certification, and a Master of Engineering from the University of Windsor deepen both sides. Independent projects across manufacturing, human resources, and media data put them together.

I am currently exploring opportunities across Data Analytics, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Electrical or Quality Engineering roles — wherever curiosity, engineering intuition, and data fluency are equally valued — and I am open to relocation.
