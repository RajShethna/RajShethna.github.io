About

From the shop floor to the dashboard

Stay Curious is more than a motto — it is the belief that data from the past can help us make wiser decisions and shape a stronger future, and it is the thread running through everything I do.

I began my career on the production floor, commissioning equipment, troubleshooting controls and analyzing plant performance as an Electrical Engineer at Reliance Industries Limited. That work created a lasting curiosity about solving problems through data rather than intuition alone.

I bring both disciplines to my work: a hands-on engineering foundation in PLC troubleshooting, root cause analysis and IATF 16949 quality systems, alongside a data practice built in SQL, Power BI, Tableau and Python. NPower's Junior Data Analyst Program, Microsoft AI-900 certification and a Master of Engineering from the University of Windsor deepen both sides.

Today I am a Manufacturing Engineer at KSR International, supporting automated assembly lines, PLC-controlled equipment and end-of-line sensor test, where curiosity, engineering intuition and data fluency meet on the shop floor every day.

Predictive Maintenance Analysis

Analyzed 10,000 production runs from the AI4I 2020 manufacturing dataset to find the process conditions behind machine failure. Found that runs above 47 Nm torque and 130 minutes of tool wear fail at 4.3x the average rate — a rule that flags the highest-risk 9.5% of runs, responsible for 41% of all failures.
